Loot system & FAQ

Flat costs. Editable class weights. Winners chosen for lasting roster value— not who shouted first or sandbagged their bags. Assignments should take about a minute.

See it in the demo Jump to FAQ
Key principle — read this first

Minimize micro-upgrades

Avoid week-to-week slot churn for tiny gains. Intelligence maintains a shared true gear state of mains and the raid force across site, addon, and AI. When a piece drops, we prefer the largest lasting upgrade for the roster—not a +1 polish for someone already ahead.

“A chain is only as strong as all its links”: upgrading 50 toons once beats upgrading one toon 50 micros.

Assignments show both the weighted victory and the upgrade delta (e.g. +30 vs +1). Micro deltas are visible and deprioritized. We also guard against sandbagging intentionally bad gear to trick the algorithm, and we do not allow double-looting the same slot without clear policy exceptions.

How loot works

1. Points

Active members receive a monthly base allotment. Additional points come from raids attended, events, and other guild-defined contributions. Points are the spend currency—not a bid.

2. Flat item costs

Every item has a fixed cost by rarity (and optionally by tier). No bidding wars. If you win, you pay the listed cost. If you don’t have the points, you’re not in the pool.

3. Best Suggested Class weights

Each item carries editable Best Suggested Class weights (e.g. Warrior 1.0, Paladin 0.85, Mage 0.1). Officers and theorycrafters can tune these over time. Only candidates at or above the weight threshold compete for that drop.

4. Winner selection (KEY)

Among best-weighted candidates who will use the item on main spec (side spec is weighted less), the winner is the one with the best combination of points standing and longest / largest lasting use—measured as upgrade delta against shared gear state. Micros lose to real upgrades.

The ~60 second assign

  1. Announce the winner — name, class, main vs side.
  2. Say why — weight, upgrade delta, points context (and who was close).
  3. Show point totals — before → after.
  4. Deduct cost — ledger updates for site / addon / AI consumers.

Live raids keep the narrative pace human; the Raid Loot Demo compresses the same logic into a fast client-side run with placeholder classic-style items.

Shared data plane

Site, in-game addon, and any AI assistant the guild uses should see the same points balances, item costs, class weights, and main-spec gear snapshots. That shared picture is what makes anti-micro and anti-sandbag rules enforceable instead of vibes-based.

FAQ

How do I earn points?
Active members get a monthly base grant. Extra points come from raid attendance, cleared bosses / lockouts as defined by officers, and occasional events. Exact rates live on the Points page once the live ledger is wired; the mock dashboard shows sample balances and history.
How do item costs work?
Costs are flat by rarity (demo: epic 40, rare 25). No bidding. You must have enough points at assign time. Costs are tunable by officers; they are not player-negotiated in raid.
Main spec vs side spec?
Main-spec use is preferred. Side-spec candidates can still appear in the pool when weight allows, but a side-spec factor reduces their score so lasting main-spec upgrades win first. The demo includes an off-spec sandbox entry to show this bias.
What is a “micro-upgrade” and why avoid it?
A micro-upgrade is a tiny slot improvement (think +1-style churn) when another raider would gain a large lasting jump on the same piece. Micros waste raid time, inflate inequality, and ignore roster strength. We show Δ prominently and deprioritize small deltas so the algorithm—and the raid lead’s announcement—favor the bigger link in the chain.
How do you stop sandbagging?
Shared true gear state means “I left greens equipped on purpose” doesn’t invent a fake +50 delta. Officers can flag anomalies; the system compares against known previous loot and expected progression. Intentional sandbagging to game weights or deltas is a guild-conduct issue, not a clever exploit.
Can someone double-loot the same slot?
Default policy: no. Once a slot is filled by a recent assign, further drops for that slot prefer other candidates unless an officer exception applies (e.g. broken item, role change). The demo focuses on first-pass assigns; double-loot guards land with the live ledger.
How does the Raid Loot Demo work?
Pure client-side JavaScript. Eight classic-style placeholder items (clearly labeled demo) drop from a fictional boss. A small roster with points, classes, and slot notes competes. “Run assign” resolves winners using points + class weight + upgrade delta (anti-micro), then shows announcement cards with before/after points and cost deducted. No backend.
What’s coming with the addon?
An in-game addon will sync attendance cues, gear snapshots, and assign announcements with the same data the site shows—so the raid lead isn’t copy-pasting between Discord and bags. Until then, the site shell and demo define the rules of the road.
