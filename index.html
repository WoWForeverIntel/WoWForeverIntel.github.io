01 · PURPOSE

What Wise Fools is for

The guild

Wise and goofy on purpose: adults who can give big answers with light energy. We welcome new and non-sweaty players without becoming a noob guild — we teach fast, build tools, and raise the floor without lowering the ceiling.

  • Adult communication — clear calls, adult humor, no ego loot theater.
  • Casual competence: success-minded without the sweat police.
  • Officers + theorycraft tune weights as real loot and trees become known.

This site

The public ops layer for the guild: explain the loot rules, show how assigns resolve, mock points and schedule, and (soon) sync with a live ledger and in-game addon so site, raid lead, and AI share one gear truth.

  • Loot FAQ — the constitution members can actually read.
  • Raid Loot Demo — eight drops, scored the way live raids will be.
  • Points & schedule shells — ready for real data when Forever settles.
02 · LOOT KERNEL

How the system thinks

PT

Points

Monthly base for active members. More from raids and events. Spend currency — not a bid.

FC

Flat cost

Rarity sets the price. Common costs less, rare costs more. No bidding wars, no stalling the pull.

WT

Class weights

Best Suggested Classes per item, editable as we learn Forever loot and talent trees.

Δ+

Longest use

Among weighted mains, prefer the largest lasting upgrade. Side-spec is downranked on purpose.

Key principle · always on

Minimize micro-upgrades

Week-to-week slot churn for tiny gains is what plagues MMO loot. Wise Fools keeps a shared true gear state across site, addon, and AI. Prefer the big upgrade for the roster chain over a +1 polish on one toon. Sandbagging gear to game Δ is a conduct problem — the system is built to see through it.

“A chain is only as strong as all its links.” Upgrade fifty toons once > one toon fifty micros.

03 · PIPELINE

From drop to announce

  1. 1
    Drop

    Raid items enter the biddable pool — site / addon / AI see the same eight (or N) pieces.

  2. 2
    Score

    Weights × points × upgrade Δ × main-spec longevity. Micros and sandbags lose signal.

  3. 3
    Announce

    ~60 seconds: winner, why, before→after points, cost deducted. Pulls keep moving.

  4. 4
    Ledger

    History and balances stay visible. No double-loot by default. Officers can audit.
