Guild pitch

  • Adult roster — communication, punctuality, and ownership of your character.
  • High-signal discourse: strategy over memes, systems over vibes.
  • Classic-feeling raid loot with modern clarity: weights, points, shared gear state.
  • Anti-micro-upgrade culture: upgrade the chain, not one link fifty times.
  • Site + addon + AI share one picture of mains and the raid force.
Key principle

Minimize micro-upgrades

Week-to-week slot churn for +1 gains burns time and morale. Prefer the largest lasting upgrade for the roster. A chain is only as strong as all its links: upgrading fifty toons once beats polishing one toon fifty times.
